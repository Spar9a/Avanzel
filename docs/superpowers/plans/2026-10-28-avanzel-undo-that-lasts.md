# 2a, Undo that lasts: Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Undo outlives the capsule card and survives a restart. Both menu Undo items come back after a quit and relaunch, and each library keeps its own. "Show the last receipt" reopens the last filing's card. ⌥⇧Z undoes the last filing from any app. The filed card says "Your exact words are kept." when the engine put them back.

**Architecture:**
- **Records.** A small store in the app's profile (`UndoRecordStore`, `undo-records.json`) keeps, per library, the commit "Undo the last filing" takes back, the last evening review's commits and (Task 3) the last filing's receipt. The controller's fields hold the open library's records only: they load as a library opens, and each change is written at once. As the library opens, git checks every saved commit (`git.inHistory`). Nothing is offered until it has, and a commit that has left HEAD's history is dropped quietly. While git can't run, nothing is offered, and the records stay on disk for a launch that has git.
- **The receipt** (`FilingReceipt`) is what the last filed or noted card showed, kept with the records. "Show the last receipt" puts it up again as a reprint: the `filed` state with `reprint`, its stamp still and dated the day it was filed, with no sound. Its Open and rows open its own pages, and its Undo is the menu's.
- **The restored line** is the `filed` state's `restored`, drawn in the card's caption slot (`Card`'s `meta`).
- **The shortcut** is a fifth `Shortcuts` key, `undo` (⌥⇧Z), registered, saved, checked and rescued with the other four. A press runs the menu's Undo; with nothing to undo, it puts up a calm bar (`nothing-to-undo`).

## Review Focus

1. **A saved Undo that history has moved past.** A reset, a rebase, a pull that rewrote history, or a library restored from a backup leaves the saved commit out of HEAD's history. It isn't offered, and its record is dropped quietly; one still in history is offered after any number of relaunches. (A revert made by hand leaves the commit in history: the Undo is offered, and git's refusal is told as `undoRefused`.) While git itself can't run, nothing is offered and no saved record is dropped: the records stay on disk, and a filing's start still clears the saved one. Task 1's tests.
2. **Library switches, mid-run too.** Each library keeps its own records, and the menu shows the open library's only. The evening Undo never reverts another library's commits. A filing or evening review that ends after its library was closed keeps its record with its own library. An Undo that waits in the queue through a switch takes back in the library it was asked in, whichever library is open by its turn, and is told once (N5, amended: it first said the Undo takes nothing back and says it can't be undone). "The same library" compares roots, never `Vault` objects: `openLibrary` makes a new one on every open. The filed card's own Undo asks in its filing's library too, which the job keeps (the final fix wave, W1). Task 1's tests.
3. **The menu rebuild budget (1d's Review Focus 3).** `filing.spec.ts:742-745` allows at most one rebuild across a filing's steps. The records' writes and the receipt add no rebuild at a filing's steps, and clearing rebuilds only when something was held. An Undo from the menu with its card gone rebuilds twice, as in 1d: `forgetFiling` takes the Undo and the receipt in one rebuild, and `tellUndone` makes the other. A `tick()` or `broadcast()` may add a third. Task 3 runs filing.spec.ts, and counts the Undo's rebuilds. A background filing builds the menu once as its note joins the queue, so a held menu hides the receipt item for its whole run (Task 3's fix round). The final fix wave adds a rebuild where the evening Undo takes its commits (W2) and where two quiet endings leave the menu as a run held it (W4); none is at a filing's steps.
4. **A reprint is no new filing.** No stamp motion, no Stamp or Drawer, no filed face (`markFiled` isn't called), no notification and no count. Its Undo is the menu's and is told once, even when it is pressed while the menu's Undo of the same filing waits (`reverting`), and so is the live card's own Undo (W1). Its Open and rows open only its own pages, and it hides when another library opens. Task 3's tests.
5. **The shortcut and the receipt never take a live card away.** Over a recording, a transcription, a filing, the editor or a problem notice, a press with nothing to undo shows nothing, and choosing "Show the last receipt" does nothing (D3, amended). With a filing to undo, ⌥⇧Z undoes once and tells once, even when pressed twice while the take-back waits.
