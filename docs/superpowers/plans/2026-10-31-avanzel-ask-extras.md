# 2b, Ask's extras: Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Ask opens with up to three suggested questions about the pages filed last. An answer can be saved as a page, with an Undo of its own. ⌥Space, pressed while Ask is the window in front, puts a spoken question in Ask's field.

**Architecture:**
- **Suggestions** are templates, with no agent run. They use setup's two sentences, one per page of the menu's "Recently filed" (the controller's `recent`), made by `suggestQuestions` beside setup's `suggestQuestion`. The window reads them, with its page count and the hint's shortcut, in one invoke, `ask:get` (`askSnapshot()`), when it opens and on each focus, and shows them only before the first question. *(Built: Task 5 folded the plan's three reads, `ask:library-pages`, `ask:suggestions` and `ask:dictate-shortcut`, into that one, as Task 2's review asked, so the shelves are counted once.)*
- **Save as a page** is the engine's save run, told the answer the person saw (`Librarian.ask(question, { save: true, answer })`, through a new `${shown}` slot in the ask prompt). Its outcome now carries its commit (`AgentOutcome.commit`). Main's `saveAnswer` runs it in the window's one run slot and answers a `SaveResult`. The turn shows its line and its ending, and an Undo that takes the commit back through 2a's `revertFiling`, in the library it was saved in. A save clears the menu's "Undo the last filing" as it starts, and again as it ends if it committed. From its press until it answers, no filing is offered for Undo (`savesGoing`, a count). *(Built: a Save waits while an Undo waits, and a turn's Undo waits while any save runs, since a commit made first can make git refuse an Undo, which has one try.)*
- **⌥Space into Ask.** `pressDictate`, while Ask is the key window, the shelves hold a page and nothing records, starts a recording flagged `forAsk`. The capsule and the transcription are a thought's, but the words are never written to the library: main pushes them to the Ask page (`ask:heard`), which adds them to the field. A hint under the field names the shortcut (read in `ask:get`) while the shelves hold a page. *(Built: with no page on a shelf there is nothing to ask about, so the window draws no hint and ⌥Space records a thought.)*

## The controller's decisions (binding, as written)

**D1. Scope.**
- **In:** three suggested questions in Ask, built from recent pages; "Save as a page" on an answer, with its own Undo; ⌥Space dictates a question into Ask's field while Ask is the focused window.
- **Out**, each named under "Not in 2b" with its reason: answers in the "reading face"; one clickable "People: Mark" chip per source; grouping sources under shelf headings; suggestions written by an agent; Ask's turns surviving a window close.

**D2. Suggested questions.**
- **Where they come from:** templates, with no agent run, from the open library's recent pages. These are the pages the menu's "Recently filed" shows (the controller's `recent`, at most 3, newest first, filings only: a page saved from Ask is not a filing and doesn't count, as today's tests pin).
- **One question per page,** de-duplicated, in that order. It uses setup's two templates (`suggestQuestion`, `setup-model.ts:181-186`): a page on the People shelf, `What am I doing with ${title}?`; any other page, `What did I say about “${title}”?`. Setup's own `suggestQuestion` keeps its behaviour. Add a sibling that makes the list, and test both.
- **Where they show:** in Ask's empty state only (no turns yet), under its existing line. They are read when the window opens and on each focus, as the page count is. *(Built: they sit in one centred column, 480 pt wide, on the axis of the centred line above them. Task 2's design review found that left-aligned rows under a centred line shared no axis with it.)*
  - Each is setup's pattern (`setup/steps/Ask.tsx:62-83`): the question as a `Text variant="callout"` sentence beside a secondary small `Button`, "Ask this", in a `role="group"` named by the sentence. A `Button` can't wrap or hold a question as its label.
  - "Ask this" asks that question exactly as Return asks a typed one.
  - With no recent pages, there are no suggestions, and the empty line stays as it is.
- **The way in:** a new invoke channel, beside `ask:library-pages`, that returns the questions. *(Amended in Task 5, as Task 2's review asked: one invoke, `ask:get`, answers `askSnapshot()`, the shelves' page count, the questions and the hint's shortcut, counted once, since `libraryPages()` stats every file of the library. `ask:library-pages` and `ask:suggestions` are gone from the bridge, because only the Ask page used them.)*

**D3. "Save as a page".**
- **What it runs:** the engine's save path, a write run that files the answer as a page the library's way (AGENTS.md: usually `topics/<slug>.md`, linked, in `index.md`, a `| ask |` log line, one commit). It is told the answer the person saw, so the page keeps those words, not a fresh answer. `Librarian.ask(question, { save: true, answer })`, or a sibling method if that reads better. `askPrompt` and its template get the shown answer in save mode. The read-only prompt is unchanged.
- **Its outcome carries its commit:** `AgentOutcome` gains `commit: string | null`, the save's commit, or the leftovers commit of a save that wrote no page or failed. Both commits exist today and their SHAs are dropped (`lib:454`, `:456`).
- **Where it's offered:** on the newest turn only, once it is answered well (as "Ask again" is on the newest failed turn). One run at a time: no Save while a question runs, and no question while a save runs. *(Amended after the pre-flight: N2. A save runs only in the library its question was answered in. Pressed after another library opened, it runs nothing and says so.)* *(Amended in Task 4's fix round: no Save, and no Save again, while an Undo waits, since a save that commits first can make git refuse that Undo, which has one try. Both buttons show their busy state and take no press. Task 5 added the mirror: while any save runs, a turn's Undo is busy and does nothing when pressed. A question is never held back by an Undo. The offer is back after a Stop and after an Undo.)*
- **What the person sees:** while it runs, the turn's own status line, with Stop; after, a success line that names the page, with "Open" and "Undo"; a save that wrote no page, failed or was stopped says so honestly, with the edits it left kept as their own commit, as a filing says. A save is an explicit press, so it doesn't ask first.
- **Its Undo** takes the save's commit back through 2a's revert path (`revertFiling(commit, library)`, the `reverting` set, the library it was asked in). It is told once, in the turn. It lives in the window's turn, so closing the window ends it (the library's history keeps the commit). *(Amended in Task 4's fix round: closing the window ends the right to ask for an Undo, not an Undo already asked for, which has left the map and takes the save back when its turn comes, untold. And the Undone line has two forms: for a page the save only changed, "Undone. “{title}” is back as it was." The library's own instructions tell an agent to update a page rather than make a near-duplicate, so a save often changes a page. Owner item 15.)*
- **The menu's "Undo the last filing"** is cleared when a save starts, as the weekly review clears it (`ctl:4072-4075`): the save commits after that filing, so its revert would be refused. "Show the last receipt" is untouched; its Undo hides with the record, as now. *(Amended after the pre-flight: FIX-1. From Save's press until the save answers, no filing is offered for Undo, by the menu, ⌥⇧Z or a reprint's Undo: a filing that ends while the save waits for the library would be offered while the save is about to commit after it. Built in Task 4 as a count, `savesGoing`: a save that ends while another goes on leaves the other's mark.)*
- **Without git, or with `auto_commit` off,** a save keeps its page and offers no Undo, as a filing does.

**D4. ⌥Space dictates a question.**
- **Which presses:** the dictate shortcut's own press (`pressDictate`), only while the Ask window is the focused window. Otherwise ⌥Space records a thought, as today. The menu's "Record a thought" (`toggleDictate`) always records a thought. Setup's `takesPress()` keeps its precedence. *(Amended in Task 5's fix round, on the controller's rulings: the press is a question's only while the shelves also hold a page. With none there is nothing to ask about, the window's own line says to say something first, and the press records a thought, with Ask in front too. `pressAsks()`, the window is key and a page is on a shelf, is the one question, asked by `pressDictate` and by `typeInstead()`: on the card a question's press brings while the speech model isn't ready, "Type instead" hides the card and stays in Ask, since the field the person was asking in is the place to type. An empty library's press was a thought's, so its "Type instead" opens Quick.)*
- **The flow:** the same recording, capsule and transcription as a thought, with the hint the capsule shows today. When the words arrive, they go into Ask's field (after whatever is typed there, with a space), and the field keeps the focus. The person presses Return to ask. A question leaves nothing in the library: no capture in the inbox, no kept audio, no `captureCount`, and no filing or evening-review path. A silent recording gets the existing "Nothing was heard." card. If the Ask window closed before the words arrive, they are dropped quietly. *(Amended after the pre-flight: FIX-8's precision. The words go only to the Ask window that was in front at the press. If it closed, they are dropped, even if another Ask window has opened since.)* Stop on a slow transcription of a question stops it and keeps nothing.
- **What tells the person:** a short hint in Ask, with the dictate shortcut as `Kbd` from `prettyAccelerator(shortcuts.dictate)`. It follows a changed shortcut, and is absent when the shortcut isn't registered. *(Amended in Task 5's fix round: and absent while the shelves hold no page, where it sat over "Your shelves are empty. Say something first…".)*
